# Thoughts on the Peaks of the Balkans Trail

I hope that you love tomatoes, cucumbers, and cheese,
because that is the main thing you'll be eating as you traverse the beautiful mountains of Kosovo, Albania, and Montenegro.
This soon-to-be classic "hut-to-hut" hike is a great way to experience a place that many Americans only know from the bleak history of the 90s.

If you want information on how to do this hike,
I always recommend [The Hiking Life](https://www.thehikinglife.com/2024/10/a-quick-dirty-guide-to-the-peaks-of-the-balkans-trail/) as a starting point for an opinionated take on it.
This post is mostly our observations after doing the hike.

## How we hiked

My wife and I hiked this trail by ourselves in July 2025,
and she did 3 days without me because I had to work in Plav.
From Plav she hiked north into Kosovo,
and then we met up at Roshkodol Pass,
a romantic meeting point after a few days apart.
I hitched from Plav up to the higher trail area on the back of a flatbed truck with some nice kids,
which was a great start to the trail.
The meetup went smoothly,
and we hiked the next 5 days together.

We hiked clockwise,
but it turns out the guidebook is written in the other direction,
and most people we met were going that way.
This was a bit of a bummer since we didn't find too many multi-day hiking buddies,
but there were a few folks we chatted with more than once.

We packed a tent because we thought it would give us more options,
but when we asked about accommodation,
it was something like 20 EUR per person for dinner, breakfast, a packed lunch, and a spot for the tent.
It was only 30 EUR per person to sleep inside,
so we ended up sleeping inside every night :)

In general the infrastructure is a little rustic,
but workable.
One night a chicken ran out of our bedroom as we opened the door,
but the room kept us dry in the rain :)

The food was basic but hearty.
The cheese was often very rustic,
but dinner would include a pasta soup or potatoes,
and was quite filling.
The language barrier with our hosts was generally pretty large,
which made the cultural side of the trip a bit less rewarding than we'd hoped.

The mountains are beautiful,
but also pretty steep.
Most days we did over 1,000m of elevation gain or loss,
but it never felt overwhelming.

## Recommendations

**Our favorite part was actually a day trip that we did before we started,
the Volušnica Loop.**
It's close to the trail,
and may be included on some routes.
We came in via Gusinje.
You hike up and around a beautiful rock ridge,
surrounded by huge peaks in every direction.
Quite a lovely experience.

We also really enjoyed the Theth to Valbona section,
though it was the most crowded.
The days into Theth and out of Valbona back into Montenegro were some of our other favorites.
But overall, every day had its highlights.

We got our border crossing permit at the police station in Plav,
which took a couple hours of confusion with a very nice cop who didn't speak any English.
Eventually we realized that he was waiting for someone else who could issue the permit,
and once that person arrived it went quickly.
I'd recommend getting it from one of the services that charge 20 EUR instead.
We never got our permits checked,
but having them removed some anxiety for a small price.

## Timing

We didn't book anything ahead of time,
and always found a place to sleep inside.
There were enough people that dinner felt social each night,
but never crowded.
I think it was the perfect time in the development of the trail to hike it,
with just enough people for our taste.

I'd recommend doing this hike in 5-6 days,
skipping most of the Kosovo part, unfortunately.
That section has a large, steep elevation drop and gain,
and some road walking.
You still cross the border a few times up high,
including walking to the tri-border summit,
so you don't fully miss Kosovo.

## Get out there

It's a perfect time to hike the trail,
while it isn't super crowded and the hosts are still excited to have you.
I'd definitely recommend it in the next few years,
because I do think there's a chance it gets more popular.
